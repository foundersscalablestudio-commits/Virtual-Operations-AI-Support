## How I Work

My focus is not simply completing tasks. I help create structure around the work so projects are easier to manage, processes are easier to repeat, and fewer operational details depend entirely on the business owner.

A typical engagement may include:

1. Reviewing current priorities, workflows, and available documentation
2. Identifying the highest-value operational work
3. Organizing and executing agreed tasks
4. Creating or improving documentation and SOPs
5. Reviewing completed work for accuracy and consistency
6. Providing clear handoff notes and recommended next steps
